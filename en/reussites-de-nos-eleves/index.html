2025-2026 school year: 100% pass rate

Baccalauréat. All three 12th grade (terminale) students passed: two with the mention Assez bien.

Diplôme national du brevet (DNB). All eight 9th grade (troisième) students passed: seven with the mention Très bien, three of them with the félicitations du jury, and one with the mention Assez bien.

Higher education. We congratulate our 12th grade students on their results and on the opportunities ahead of them:

  • one student was admitted, as their first choice, to the MPSI preparatory class (mathematics, physics and engineering sciences) at the Lycée naval de Brest;
  • one student, admitted to the hypokhâgne preparatory class (humanities) at the Lycée Molière, chose to study at Old Dominion University and to enroll in the online law degree of Université Paris-Panthéon-Assas;
  • one student, eligible for the BCPST preparatory class in Boulogne-Billancourt, chose to study at Old Dominion University.

FASN congratulates all its graduates and wishes them every success in this new stage of their journey.

2024-2025 school year

Baccalauréat. All seven 12th grade students passed the baccalauréat: three with the mention Très bien, one of them with the félicitations du jury, one with the mention Bien and one with the mention Assez bien.

Diplôme national du brevet (DNB). All ten 9th grade students passed the DNB: five with the mention Très bien and four with the mention Bien.

Higher education (Parcoursup). The seven 12th grade students were admitted to the following programs:

  • two students to scientific preparatory classes, at the Lycée Chaptal (Paris) and the Lycée Chateaubriand (Rennes);
  • one student to medical school, in Dijon;
  • one student to a double degree in French law and common law, at Université Paris Nanterre and the University of Essex;
  • one student to the business school of Old Dominion University (ODU), in Norfolk (United States);
  • one student to a nursing school (IFSI), at the Limoges University Hospital;
  • one student to an engineering school, EPITA (Paris).

Returning to France

On their return, FASN students also join prestigious private high schools. At the request of the schools concerned, FASN is authorized to hold admission test sessions for their applicants, such as Grandchamps, the École internationale de Saint-Cloud and the Lycée Saint-Louis-de-Gonzague.
