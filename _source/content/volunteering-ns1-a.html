Students and parents are invited to volunteer within the French American School of Norfolk (FASN).

Goals

Volunteering is a way to get to know the school better and to actively support the students. It also makes it possible to propose activities in partnership with Norfolk Public Schools (NPS) and Virginia Beach City Public Schools (VBCPS).

How students can volunteer

Students can:

  • tutor American students who are learning French;
  • help organize FASN events, in particular the art competition and Candlemas (la Chandeleur), organized with the Alliance française.

What students gain

  • Students give a few hours of their time and enthusiasm.
  • They meet and talk with French families outside FASN, as well as with American families from Norfolk and Virginia Beach.
  • Every student volunteer receives a volunteering certificate.
  • These commitments are among the important elements of Parcoursup and Common App applications.

How parents can volunteer

Parents can:

  • help organize FASN events, in particular the potlucks;
  • proctor mock exams;
  • become parents’ representatives;
  • organize fundraisers;
  • stand for election to the Board of Directors.
