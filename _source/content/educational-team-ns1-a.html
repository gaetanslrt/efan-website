Who are the teachers of the French curriculum at FASN?

For the 2026-2027 school year, the teachers of the French curriculum come from the French Ministry of National Education or from higher education: primary school teachers, holders of the CAPES or the agrégation, and university lecturers. They work at FASN either on secondment from the Ministry of National Education or as locally recruited staff, according to their training, teaching experience and field of expertise.

How the teaching team works

The teaching team meets once a month in a teachers’ council (conseil pédagogique) and three times a year in class councils (conseils de classe).

Who are the teachers of the American curriculum at FASN?

As FASN students are integrated into regular American classes, their teachers for the American curriculum are the American teachers of the schools that host FASN.
