The FASN Board of Directors

The French American School of Norfolk (FASN) is a non-profit organization governed by a Board of Directors. The Board oversees financial management, recruits the school’s Director and ensures the consistency of its strategy.

Membership

The Board of Directors has six voting members:

  • the President;
  • the Vice-President;
  • the Treasurer;
  • the Secretary;
  • the representative of the Élément de soutien national (ESN);
  • the representative of the Mission laïque française (MLF).

It also includes five non-voting members, who serve in an advisory capacity.

How the Board works

The Board meets every month to discuss and vote on a range of matters, including the budget, expenditure, recruitment, and the application and updating of the school’s regulations.

The role of the Mission laïque française

The Mission laïque française, a non-profit operator of the French State, is mandated to support the school’s educational and academic operation, in consultation with the Board of Directors and the school’s leadership. It acts to:

  • recruit teachers;
  • provide ongoing teacher training tailored to the school’s specific context;
  • monitor the quality of teaching during regular visits.
