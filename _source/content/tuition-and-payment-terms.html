When is registration complete?

Please note that registration is considered complete when both of the following conditions are met:

  • all required documents have been completed and submitted to the school administration;
  • the applicable fees have been paid.

Registration fees

  • For applications submitted before May 31, the registration fee is $275 for a new student and $140 for re-enrollment.
  • For applications submitted on or after June 1, the registration fee increases by $15 per month until the start of the school year.

Refunds

Please note that registration fees are non-refundable.

For more information, please see the FASN financial regulations (in French).
