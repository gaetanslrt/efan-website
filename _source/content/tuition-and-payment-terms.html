Registration is considered complete once all required documents have been completed, submitted to the school administration, and the applicable fees have been paid:

  • Registration fees are $275 per new student and $140 for re-enrollment for applications submitted before May 31.
  • For applications submitted on or after June 1, the registration fee increases by $15 per month until the start of the school year.

Please note that all fees are non-refundable.
