Educational goals

As part of the moral and civic education (EMC), history and HGGSP curricula, students reflect on several notions, including democracy, equality and the protection of democracies. To explore these notions in depth, all students attend talks given by NATO military personnel, French or of other nationalities, and discuss them with the speakers.

These conferences were created in 2025 by Mrs. Auvray, FASN’s history-geography and HGGSP teacher.

Topics for 2026-2027

  • protecting democracies: the fight against terrorism (part 1);
  • gender equality: how it is put into practice in the French armed forces;
  • defense and national security: understanding the notion of security through the French White Paper on Defence and National Security;
  • protecting democracies: the fight against terrorism (part 2).

Calendar

To give students time to develop their thinking and prepare their work in the best conditions, the talks take place mainly in November.

Students concerned

Students in 9th, 10th, 11th and 12th grade (troisième, seconde, première and terminale) attend these talks. All of them will indeed reflect on these topics, this year or the next, as part of the EMC curriculum and its exams.
