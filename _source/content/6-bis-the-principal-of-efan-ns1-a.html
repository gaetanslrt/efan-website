The FASN Director

The Director of the French American School of Norfolk leads with vision and purpose, overseeing the school’s daily operations and advancing its distinctive bilingual and bicultural mission. Responsible for the same areas as a school principal in France, the Director also serves as the school’s pedagogical advisor, recruiting and evaluating teachers in collaboration with the Mission Laïque Française. Working in partnership with NPS and VBCPS, the Director ensures that FASN flourishes within the American educational landscape while honoring the excellence of French pedagogy. By collaborating closely with parent representatives, teachers, and the wider community, the Director fosters innovation, excellence, and a spirit of collaboration. Through dynamic curriculum leadership, staff empowerment, and meaningful community connections, the Director helps students grow into confident, globally minded learners and strengthens the vibrant, inclusive community that defines FASN.

The Accountant

The Accountant of the French American School of Norfolk plays a key role in ensuring the school’s financial health and operational excellence. Overseeing all aspects of budgeting, accounting, and financial reporting, they provide strategic insight that supports the school’s mission and long-term sustainability. Working closely with the Director, the Accountant ensures transparency and compliance within both French and American accounting frameworks. They manage payroll, tuition, and financial audits with precision and integrity. Through their expertise and attention to detail, the Accountant helps maintain the school’s stability and growth—supporting an environment where students, teachers, and families can thrive.
