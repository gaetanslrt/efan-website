The FASN School Council is a forum for dialogue between families and the school. It meets every term and brings together the parents’ representatives, the teachers, the Director and a representative of the Mission laïque française (MLF), to review parents’ feedback and questions as well as the school’s various activities.

The parents’ representatives then share the minutes of each School Council meeting with parents.

Please note: individual situations are not discussed at the School Council. They are handled directly between the parents, the teacher concerned and, if necessary, the Director.
