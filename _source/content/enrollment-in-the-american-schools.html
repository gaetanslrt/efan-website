Enrollment in the American schools is separate from enrollment at FASN. It is your responsibility: you complete all the steps yourself.

Enrollment in five steps

Pre-registration can be done online, but the registration itself takes place in person, by appointment.

Step 1: gather your proofs of residence

To enroll your child, you must present two proofs of residence. You therefore need to have a home before starting the process.

Step 2: complete the registration file (pre-registration)

The file can be completed online or on paper. Online registration is, however, only possible under certain conditions. To obtain the file, you can:

  • download it from the school’s or the city’s website (links below);
  • or pick it up at the school office during your visit to the school.

Step 3: provide the French report cards

Schools ask for your children’s French report cards. Virginia Beach City Public Schools (VBCPS) now asks for them to be provided in French. They are then translated by the VBCPS World Language Office. You therefore no longer need to have them translated or certified.

Step 4: complete the medical examination

A medical examination is required before any registration appointment. It is carried out by an American doctor, who validates the vaccinations and helps you complete the health and physical forms.

Step 5: finalize registration in person, by appointment

Once all the previous steps are complete, you finalize registration by going to the school with your child. The American school gives you an appointment (for each school’s contact details, see the “American schools” tab). For some grades, you will meet with the counselor, who draws up your child’s timetable.
