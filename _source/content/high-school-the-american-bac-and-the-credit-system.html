What graduation means

In the United States, earning the high school diploma is called “graduation”: the student “graduates”.

At Cox High School, students can work towards:

  • the standard diploma, which requires 22 credits;
  • the advanced diploma, which requires 26 credits.

These 22 and 26 credits are called “standard units of credit”.

Verified credits and SOL tests

To graduate, students must also earn five verified credits. A verified credit is awarded when the student has successfully completed a 140-hour course and passed the corresponding SOL (Standards of Learning) test. The five SOL tests to pass are:

  • two English tests, in reading and in writing, from 11th grade (première);
  • one mathematics test;
  • one science test;
  • one history and social studies test.

In short:

  • the standard diploma requires 22 standard units of credit and five verified credits;
  • the advanced diploma requires 26 standard units of credit and five verified credits.

The diploma with honors

The diploma with honors is awarded to students whose grade point average (GPA) is 3.0 or higher.

How do students earn the 22 or 26 credits required?

  • Credits are earned between 9th grade (troisième) and 12th grade (terminale). Each American course completed with a satisfactory average earns the corresponding credit, usually one credit.
  • Some courses taken at FASN earn credits under agreements between Virginia Beach City Public Schools (VBCPS) and FASN: three mathematics credits (Algebra 1, Algebra 2 and Geometry) and one history credit (World History 1).
  • Schooling completed in France can also earn credits. This is the principle of credit equivalence.

How is credit equivalence applied?

Based on the French report cards, a central VBCPS office grants each student credits for the subjects with sufficient results. On that basis, the counselor draws up a personalized timetable with the subjects still needed to reach the required number of credits.

It is therefore important to provide all French report cards as soon as you arrive in Norfolk. VBCPS now asks for these report cards to be provided in French: they are then translated by the VBCPS World Language Office.

Graduating without completing all four years

Students can earn the high school diploma even if they arrive in 10th grade (seconde), 11th grade (première) or 12th grade (terminale). They then need to gather the required number of credits, in particular through credit equivalence for their schooling in France and the courses taken in the United States.

A student who has enough credits in 11th grade (première) can graduate from high school. They can then no longer take FASN classes in 12th grade (terminale).
