American curriculum

FASN students take the same assessments as American students, with results expressed as letter grades. As long as their level of English is not sufficient, these assessments come with support measures.

During their first year as ESL students, their American results are expressed as “Pass” or “Fail”. This way of grading eases their adjustment by limiting the pressure of grades.

Assessments are done on paper or digitally. Results can be viewed on the ParentVUE platform, and an American report card is issued for each grading period.

French curriculum

Assessments are designed and marked by the FASN teacher.

At the end of each term, a school report (livret scolaire) is sent to families. It shows the results obtained in both the French and the American curricula, the French teacher’s comments and the opinion of the FASN Director. A Livret scolaire unique (LSU) is also issued at the end of the school year. All these documents are useful when returning to France.
