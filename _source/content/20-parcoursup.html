EFAN students can apply on Parcousup and follow the same calendar as any candidates who stayed in France. 

However, the data (grades, assessments, etc.) considered on Parcoursup are not the same as for students in France (see the comparison table below), as the student is registered on Parcoursup as a “free candidate studying abroad.”  

EFAN provides guidance on the registration process.

Comparative table Parcoursup in France / at FASN

Remarks : 

  • The continuous assessment grades from Première are replaced by final exam grades. Since the student enrolled at EFAN is registered as an “individual candidate” for the Baccalaureate and as a “candidate attending school abroad” on Parcoursup, the continuous assessment grades from Première are not applicable. Note: A student who chooses to take only the French exams in Première will not have continuous assessment grades to be reported on Parcoursup.  
  • For more details, refer to the “Baccalaureate” tab. 

The ability to follow a dual curriculum is a key strength to highlight in the Parcoursup application. This is supported by the EFAN report cards, which include both French and American grades, as well as the American report card.
