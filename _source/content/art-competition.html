Art Competition

Participate in the exciting art competition organized by the students of the French American School of Norfolk! This year’s artworks will celebrate the 400 years of the French Navy in the context of the US 250, honoring its rich history and enduring legacy. All submitted pieces will be exhibited at the prestigious Harrison Opera House on April 12, 2026. Join us in celebrating creativity, talent, and heritage as we showcase the artistic vision of emerging young artists.
