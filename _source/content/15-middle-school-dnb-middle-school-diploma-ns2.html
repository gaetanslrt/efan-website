Preparation

FASN teachers, who follow the curriculum published in the Ministry of National Education’s Bulletin officiel (BO), together with CNED materials, prepare 9th grade (troisième) students for the Diplôme national du brevet. In particular, students sit written mock exams under the same conditions as in France.

Registration

FASN holds an information meeting to explain how to register for the exams on the Cyclades platform.

Independent candidate status (candidat libre or candidat individuel)

FASN students are registered for the DNB as independent candidates. In France, continuous assessment grades count towards the DNB. This is not the case for independent candidates: the grades earned during the year in the FASN French curriculum are not taken into account. For each subject concerned, continuous assessment is replaced by an exam held at the end of the year.

These official exams take place at the Lycée Rochambeau, in Bethesda.

Their calendar differs from that of France: they take place in early June.

The exams

Students take the same written exams as candidates schooled in France:

  • French;
  • history-geography and moral and civic education;
  • mathematics;
  • two subjects chosen from the following three: physics-chemistry, life and earth sciences, or technology.

The two subjects of the science exam are announced to families in April by the exam coordinator for the United States.

Students also take a written exam in a modern language.
