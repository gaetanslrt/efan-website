Role

Parents’ representatives are elected every year. They act as the link between parents and the school: they gather and pass on the comments, opinions and questions of the school community.

They sit on the School Council, where they report:

  • to the teaching team, the questions and suggestions of the parents they represent about how the school runs;
  • to parents, the educational initiatives proposed by the Director and the teachers, and the general state of the school.

They are also responsible for sharing the minutes of School Council meetings with parents.

The School Council meets every term. Its meetings bring together the parents’ representatives, the teachers, the Director and a representative of the Mission laïque française (MLF), to review parents’ feedback and questions as well as the school’s various activities.

Please note: individual situations are handled directly between the parents, the teacher concerned and, if necessary, the Director.

Applications and elections

The Director issues a call for candidates at the end of September. Parents who wish to become representatives send their application by email to the FASN Director. Parents then elect their representatives through a vote held with an online form (Google Forms).
