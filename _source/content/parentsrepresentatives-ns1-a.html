Role

Parent representatives communicate with parents and pass on comments, opinions or questions from the school community.
They participate in the school board. 

They report :
– to parents: educational initiatives proposed by the principal and teachers, the general state of the school, etc;
– to the teaching staff: questions and/or suggestions on school operations from the parents they represent.

They are also responsible for communicating school council minutes to the community.

PLEASE NOTE: Questions about specific cases are dealt with directly between the parents, the teacher(s) concerned and the principal, if necessary.

How to become a parent representative

Submit your application by e-mail to the FASN management.
