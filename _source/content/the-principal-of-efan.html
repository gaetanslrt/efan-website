The Director

The Director of the French American School of Norfolk (FASN) leads the school and oversees its daily operation. She carries out the school’s bilingual and bicultural mission. She holds the same responsibilities as a school principal in France and also acts as the school’s pedagogical advisor: she recruits and evaluates teachers, in collaboration with the Mission laïque française (MLF).

Working in partnership with Norfolk Public Schools (NPS) and Virginia Beach City Public Schools (VBCPS), she ensures that FASN is integrated into the American educational framework while guaranteeing teaching quality in line with French pedagogy. She maintains regular dialogue with the parents’ representatives, the teachers and the school’s partners.

She provides the school’s educational leadership, supports and values the staff, and develops relationships with the school community. In doing so, she contributes to student success and to the inclusive school climate that makes FASN so rich.

The Accountant

The FASN Accountant is responsible for the school’s financial management. She oversees the preparation and monitoring of the budget, the bookkeeping and the production of financial reports, with the aim of ensuring the school’s long-term sustainability.

She works closely with the Director and ensures that the accounts are transparent and comply with both French and American accounting frameworks. She manages payroll, tuition and financial audits.
