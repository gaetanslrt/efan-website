2025-2026 Higher Education Destinations

We are proud to celebrate the outstanding achievements of our graduating class and the exciting opportunities they have earned.

  • One student was admitted to and will attend the prestigious MPSI (Mathematics, Physics, and Engineering Sciences) preparatory program at the Lycée Naval de Brest, his first-choice program.
  • One student earned admission to the highly selective Hypokhâgne (humanities) preparatory program at Lycée Molière and chose to pursue higher education at Old Dominion University instead.
  • Another student, eligible for admission to the competitive BCPST (Biology, Chemistry, Physics, and Earth Sciences) preparatory program in Boulogne-Billancourt, also chose to continue their academic journey atOld Dominion University.

We congratulate all of our graduates on their remarkable accomplishments and wish them every success as they embark on this exciting new chapter.
