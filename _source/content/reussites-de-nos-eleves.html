2024-2025 school year

Higher education (Parcoursup). The seven 12th grade students were admitted to the following programs:

  • one student to medical school, in Dijon;
  • one student to a double degree in French law and common law, at Université Paris Nanterre and the University of Essex;
  • one student to the business school of Old Dominion University (ODU), in Norfolk (United States);
  • one student to a nursing school (IFSI), at the Limoges University Hospital;
  • one student to an engineering school, EPITA (Paris).

Returning to France

On their return, FASN students also join prestigious private high schools. At the request of the schools concerned, FASN is authorized to hold admission test sessions for their applicants, such as Grandchamps, the École internationale de Saint-Cloud and the Lycée Saint-Louis-de-Gonzague.
